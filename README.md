Pope Francis has issued a compelling warning about the ethical pitfalls of artificial intelligence during a G-7 conference. Highlighting AI’s dual nature—offering immense benefits while threatening societal dehumanization—he stressed the need for ethical boundaries to prevent exploitation by technocratic systems. His remarks underscore the importance of maintaining human control over AI decisions to preserve dignity and avoid a future dominated by machines. As developers, we must consider these ethical implications in our work to ensure AI serves humanity responsibly.
